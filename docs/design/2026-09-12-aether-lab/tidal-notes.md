Art direction: a low camera over a broad, displaced spectral sea. A large eclipsed sun opens a reflection path across the water. Stratified clouds, an auroral edge, sparse fixed stars and three irregular coastline layers give the horizon depth. Wave displacement and analytic reflection normals share one field. Broken crest filigree replaces regular wire grids. Six bounded real-onset slots launch expanding surface interference fronts.

Silk is the ordered tide, Nova raises a storm and denser cloud layers, and Helix introduces spiral currents. Flow advances water/cloud advection; Spread changes wavelength; Turbulence alters swell and small reflection ripples; Glow shapes the eclipse and luminous crests; Depth alters atmospheric extinction and auroral visibility; Focus narrows the specular highlight. Journey remains host-owned. Global particle density and glow affect luminous details; global exposure, grade and bloom remain host-owned. Frame bass/mid/treble/energy and onset/pulse are supplied by the real shared audio engine.

The scene deliberately uses aspectFit:false and orbit:false, targeting [0,3.10,-8] in world space. Its group is positioned at y=2.75 by the host. The ocean extends ±64, and the inside sky has radius68; far water fades to its exact background ray. The soft displacement envelope keeps crests below the authored camera at every supported zoom distance. High uses 193² vertices, eight waves and three noise octaves; Low uses 81² vertices, five waves and two noise octaves. No own renderer, timer, texture, context or audio.
