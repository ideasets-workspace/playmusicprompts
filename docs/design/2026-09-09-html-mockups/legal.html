What we collect

An anonymous listener id (a cookie), the prompts you type, the tracks you play, like and share, and technical logs needed to run the service.

Why we collect it

To compose and deliver your music, to keep one listening queue across pages, to measure what works, and to show ads that fund the free service.

Ads and consent

Consent is asked region by region: opt-in where the law requires it (Türkiye, EEA, UK, Switzerland), default-on with a one-tap opt-out elsewhere. Your choice is remembered and changeable at any time from “Cookies & ads”.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, portability and objection — write to the contact address above.

Last updated 2026-09-04

The service

PlayMusicPrompts composes music from your description and lets you listen, save and share. It is free and has no credits or paid tiers.

Generated music and rights

Tracks are generated. Commercial use and sync are permitted; copyright is not warranted. Each track carries a machine-readable provenance mark (EU AI Act Art. 50).

Acceptable use

Prompts are content-checked before anything is composed; requests that fail the policy are refused with the reason shown.

Cookies & ads

Last updated 2026-09-04

Strictly necessary

The listener id and your consent choice. Always on.

Analytics

GA4 with Consent Mode v2 — regional defaults are set on the server before any tag runs.

Advertising

Audio ad breaks between tracks (never inside one) and one banner slot above the player. Every ad is marked as an ad, with who paid for it (DSA Art. 26).
