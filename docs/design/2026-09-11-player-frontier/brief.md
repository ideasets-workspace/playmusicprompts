## Art direction and acceptance

- Neural Bloom (existing aurora id/module/export): a sprawling living network/tree of luminous branching fibers, electrical impulses travelling from trunk into fine branches, suspended cellular nodes and deep particle atmosphere. Organic fractal silhouette with actual 3D depth, large enough to inhabit the space. Quiet violet/teal with restrained pearly glints; not old curtains or an anonymous glowing sphere.
- Event Horizon (existing orbital id/module/export): an immersive black-hole world with a black central void, tilted flowing accretion disk, convincing curved light/lensing illusion and deep stars. Music excites disk turbulence and outgoing fronts. Cinematic dark space and hot restrained spectral light; not mechanical gimbals or a collection of thin circles. This is visual art, not a scientific simulation claim.
- Prism Passage (existing liquid id/module/export): an architectural journey through a deep, curving prismatic tunnel. Receding polygonal frames/faceted surfaces, spectral light rails, travelling pulses, impossible depth. High quality luminous glass/black chrome structure with coherent perspective; not a chrome blob or turntable sculpture. Flight/crawl speeds depend on audio energy with gentle idle movement and exact motion freeze.
- Each asset must have an attractive static initial pose, explicit motion freeze (including audio brightness), local phase continuity, all nine palette support and clean disposal. No network, fake beat clock, embedded music or own animation loop.
- Reuse the exact host Three namespace. Factory returns {group, update(frame), setPalette(world), dispose, diagnostics?, view?}. Host positions group at y=2.75 and hides the Record floor/architecture/dust for alternatives so they own their visual world. Optional view={target:[worldX,worldY,worldZ], direction:[x,y,z], distance:number, fitRadius:number, minDistance:number,maxDistance:number, orbit:boolean, bloom:number}. Default target[0,2.75,0], direction[.1,.04,1], distance10, fitRadius3.2. Tunnel may use a bespoke camera profile. Do not mutate camera/render/audio/UI directly. Host manages aspect fitting. Declare visibility bounds for shader-deformed objects.
- frame: existing {time,dt,bass,mid,treble,energy,motion,intensity,active,spinVelocity}, plus {pulse:0..1 onset envelope, onset:boolean one frame, centroid:0..1 log spectral brightness, spectrum:Float32Array(64) normalized log bands, quality:'low'|'high'|'auto'}. Clamp input; drive actual reaction from these values. Idle motion may be slow, but never fabricate onsets. Motionfalse freezes pose/material animation; explicit palette/intensity/quality changes are allowed.
- Textureless procedural geometry/shading, bounded resources; target at most30drawcalls and150k triangles per asset, no heavy per-frame allocation. GPU shader deformation with correct normals preferred. Shader compilation and actual browser rendered review by parent are mandatory before acceptance; Node structural tests alone are insufficient.
