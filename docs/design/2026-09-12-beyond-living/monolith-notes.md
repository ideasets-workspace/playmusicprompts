## What changed

The accepted canyon, stepped floor, deep aperture, lighting and contrast curve are retained. The added composition sits around local `(0, 2.3, -17.5)`, suspended above a solid stepped foundation and connected visually to side pylons. The existing camera places it in the middle of the inhabited space, rather than in a separate scene.

- Twelve individually articulated, beveled mineral leaves form two offset mechanical layers.
- Three complete hexagonal truss levels, pivot sockets, bronze inlays and fine emissive etchings create thickness and silhouette detail.
- Twelve barrels and twelve narrow piston rods connect actual shared start joints to moving blade endpoints. A numeric test checks the endpoints from the real matrices.
- Machined outer lamellae and small fasteners subdivide the silhouette. Nine separate elongated crystal fragments form the focal center; the light occupies their gaps.
- The leaves open and orient toward intentional pointer input. Audio bass and the existing traveling onset front add coherent articulation. The existing three form IDs alter the machinery alongside the canyon: nova opens it further; helix adds a crossed tilt and asymmetric leaf articulation.
- Sparse light shafts integrate density along view rays through a bounded box. Twenty-eight steps at normal/high quality become fourteen at Low. Their RGB contribution is capped at `.045` and disabled at depth zero, preserving the accepted contrast treatment.

## Touch, motion and controls

`frame.touch` reads bounded finite x/y/strength/serial values; missing input returns smoothly to neutral. Host strength decay is respected after `active:false`; it is not discarded prematurely. Touch works while audio is paused, and it never schedules a musical onset. Motion off skips all touch smoothing and preserves the last animated orientation, opening, shader state and instance matrices even when the host sends neutral input or a new serial. Static form/visual edits remain effective as in the accepted asset.

All prior lab/global controls remain connected. Flow owns ambient phase, spread opens the canyon and adjusts the machinery radius, turbulence affects structure and shafts, radiance changes existing luminous finishes, depth changes haze and shafts, and focus changes the aperture/shaft concentration. Real audio remains gated by `frame.active`. There is no new renderer, audio context, animation loop or network dependency.
