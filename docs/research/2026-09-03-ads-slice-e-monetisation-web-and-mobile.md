# Outcome first

1. Google is the only stack that can be integrated without a sales conversation: AdSense (web) + AdMob (apps) via `google_mobile_ads` 9.1.0 (published 2026-08-12) [S06][S07]. Both require, for EEA/UK/Swiss traffic, a Google-CERTIFIED CMP integrated with IAB TCF to serve personalised ads — since 16 Jan 2024 (EEA/UK) and 31 Jul 2024 (CH) [S01]. Our custom GA4 consent banner is NOT a certified CMP; without one, EEA traffic is limited to non-personalised/limited ads [S01]. Cheapest compliant route: Google's own "Privacy & messaging" European regulations message (Google LLC CMP, TCF ID 300, web AND app) which Google lists as certified [S01]; in Flutter it is the UMP SDK (`ConsentInformation.instance.requestConsentInfoUpdate` → `ConsentForm.loadAndShowConsentFormIfRequired` → `canRequestAds`) [S05].
2. AdSense eligibility is stated only as: own content that is high-quality, original, attracts an audience; HTML-source access; 18+ [S04]. The player page's thinness is a real "low value content" risk that Google's official pages do not quantify [see § Contradictions]; mitigation = real text content (about/how-it-works/FAQ/catalogue pages) before applying. `[INFERENCE]` from S04 + third-party rejection reports [S38][S39] — these are secondary and do not count as verification.
3. AdMob: account review "typically up to 24 hours, rarely up to 2 weeks"; ads do not serve and the app is not reviewed until payment info is added; app must be in a supported store and pass an app readiness review; apps created after January 2025 without a verified app-ads.txt face LIMITED ad serving [S15]. Interstitials may not fire on app load/exit, not after every action (max 1 per 2 user actions), never in background, and only at logical breaks [S03] — for a music player the lawful break is the TRACK BOUNDARY, never mid-track and never during the T−90 s recalibration.
4. "Generate one more track free" as a REWARDED ad is permitted: the reward is an in-app, non-transferable, non-monetary item; the user must opt in affirmatively, skipping must not impede normal use, and the promised reward must be delivered [S16]. Rewarded interstitial variant needs an intro screen with a "no" option [S16]. Server-side verification (SSV) callbacks exist for validating each completed view before we spend $0.08 [S17].
5. Payout to Türkiye: AdSense/AdMob payment threshold ₺200 (TRY accounts) or $100 (USD accounts); verification threshold $10-equivalent; payment-method selection ₺20 [S02][S17b]; payments issued 21st–26th if the balance on the 20th meets the threshold; wire transfers may take up to 15 business days [S09]; Türkiye payment options: EFT Yes, Wire Yes, Check No, Hyperwallet No; bank must be in the same country as the payment address [S10 PARTIAL].
6. Listener tolerance is measured, not guessed: the Pandora 21-month randomised experiment on ~35 M listeners found each additional audio ad per hour cut listening hours by 2.082 % ± 0.227 % and active days by 1.911 % ± 0.130 %; the long-run elasticity (≈ −0.070 on hours) is ~3× the first-month estimate; listeners respond to total ads/hour and slightly prefer MORE frequent but SHORTER breaks; realised load spanned 2.7–5.0 ads/hour [S08 FULL]. For PlayMusicPrompts: one ad per track boundary (~60 s tracks) would be ~60 ads/hour — 12–20× Pandora's range — and is therefore not a viable audio-ad cadence; the cadence must be per N tracks or per minutes listened.
7. Break-even (CALCULATION, §5): a free listener who triggers G paid generations costs 0.08·G USD; ad revenue = Σ impressions × eCPM/1000. With one rewarded view per generation, the break-even rewarded eCPM is $80 — above every benchmark range found (rewarded $8–$30 global/Tier-1, secondary sources) — so ads alone cannot pay for one-take-per-generation; reuse of catalogue tracks (the parent's other slices) plus a rewarded gate on EXTRA generations is the only arithmetic that closes.

The parent must read the current AdMob Android/iOS quick-start pages before pinning.
- Formats: the plugin supports banner (incl. adaptive), interstitial, rewarded, rewarded interstitial, native, app open (plugin description S06; release notes S07 name AdManagerBannerAd and preloading). Which of these fit a music player is governed by S03 and S16 below.
- Interstitial rules (S03 [FULL]): never on app load or exit (use app open ads instead); never while the app runs in the background; at most one interstitial per two user actions; never immediately after another interstitial; only at logical breaks; pre-load to avoid late pop-ups. Mapping to Berk's algorithm: the only lawful interstitial moment is the TRACK BOUNDARY after the user's own action (skip or "next"); auto-advance boundaries while the phone is locked are "background" and forbidden. Background audio + ads: AdMob's rule is that ads must not be placed in apps running in the background (S03) — so no visual ad may be shown, and no interstitial may fire, while playback continues with the screen off; audio-only ad insertion is outside AdMob's format set (see Item 4).
- Rewarded "generate one more track free" (S16 [FULL]): allowed — an extra generation is an indirect, non-transferable, in-app reward. Conditions: clear disclosure of action + reward before each ad; affirmative opt-in (button), skippable; the reward must be delivered on completion; publishers may not say "watch this ad to support us"; a rewarded-interstitial variant needs an intro screen with a functional "no" option. Interaction with the paid $0.08 generation: reward should be granted client-side immediately and validated with SSV callbacks (S17 [PARTIAL], S17d [PARTIAL]: Google retries the callback up to five times at 1 s; cache the public key <=24 h) so the spend ledger records ad-funded takes separately from quota takes.

The messages themselves (GDPR, and the iOS ATT explainer) are created in AdMob → Privacy & messaging.

## Item 4 — Audio-native monetisation for a generative music player

- Listener tolerance, primary evidence (S08 [FULL], five-part record below): demand falls linearly in ads/hour; each extra ad/hour costs 2.08 % of hours and 1.91 % of active days over 21 months; the long-run elasticity (about -0.070 hours, -0.076 days) is roughly 3x the first-month value; listeners react to TOTAL ads/hour, with a slight preference for more frequent, shorter breaks; higher ad load raised paid ad-free subscriptions, especially among older listeners. Realised load in the experiment was 2.7-5.0 audio ads/hour (Table 3).
- Spotify free-tier ad frequency: NO Spotify first-party number was found this session; the only figures are a secondary blog (2-6 ads/hour, S57 [UNVERIFIED]) and a small Indonesian survey (S42 [PARTIAL]: 65 % name ad-free listening as the top upgrade reason; 44 of 148 respondents switched services). Recorded as an absence.
- Fit with Berk's algorithm: tracks are ~60 s median. One ad per track boundary = ~60 ad opportunities/hour, 12-20x the Pandora range; S08's linear demand curve implies that cadence would destroy listening. Ad slot at T-90 s (mid-track) is worse: it is the "unexpectedly launch while focused on a task" case forbidden for interstitials (S03) and is mid-content for audio. Recommended cadence unit: minutes listened (e.g. one break per 12-20 minutes, i.e. 3-5/hour, the measured Pandora band) placed at a track boundary that follows a full listen, never after an early skip (which is a strong-reject signal in Berk's algorithm and stacking an ad on it compounds the negative).
- Companion banners: AdsWizz shows they are standard in stream ads but carry a cookie/GDPR side effect (S25). For the web player a companion 300x250 in the player panel during an audio break is feasible; for the app, AdMob banner formats can serve as companions only while the app is foregrounded (S03).
- "Sponsored generation" (rewarded ad before a paid generation): permitted under S16; it is also the ONLY format whose value per impression is in the same order of magnitude as the $0.08 take cost (Item 5). Skip behaviour: rewarded ads must remain skippable (S16); an early skip forfeits the reward but must not impede normal use.

## Item 5 — Revenue expectations and break-even against $0.08 per take

Primary numbers: NO first-party eCPM benchmark table was found this session from Google, AppLovin or Unity (S32 has none; Q10 yield). All eCPM ranges below are SECONDARY ESTIMATES (S33, S34, S35) and are labelled so; they agree in order of magnitude, which is the only thing this section relies on: rewarded video Tier-1 $12-30, global $8-18; interstitial Tier-1 $5-13, global $2.5-5; banner $0.20-1.50. Türkiye-specific eCPMs: none found in any source — absence recorded; treat TR as below the "global average" band `[INFERENCE]`.

Break-even model (CALCULATION, all inputs sourced): let G = paid generations per free listener-session (cost 0.08 G USD, CONTEXT-03), I_f = impressions of format f per session, e_f = eCPM in USD. Revenue R = sum_f I_f * e_f / 1000. Break-even requires R >= 0.08 G.
- One rewarded impression per generation (I_rw = G): e_rw >= 80 USD/1000 — no benchmark reaches this; rewarded alone covers 10-37 % of a take at the estimate bands ($8-30).
- Banner refreshing every 60 s during a 20-minute session (I_b = 20) at $0.50: R = $0.01 — covers 12.5 % of ONE take.
- Interstitial every 4 tracks in a 20-track session (I_i = 5) at $4 global: R = $0.02; at $10 Tier-1: $0.05.
- Therefore, with G = 1 per session (Berk's playlist needs 3 takes up front, so G >= 3 for a fresh session = $0.24 cost), ad revenue at any benchmark band is 1/10 to 1/3 of cost. Sensitivity: revenue scales linearly with eCPM and impressions; cost scales linearly with G. The lever that closes the gap is G: catalogue REUSE (the parent's other slices) drives G toward 0 for repeat sessions, and the rewarded gate ("watch to generate one more") converts the marginal generation into an opt-in with ~$0.01-0.03 of revenue attached. Formula for the parent: break-even eCPM_rw = 80 * (G_paid / I_rw); with 1 rewarded per 4 paid takes it is $320; with reuse ratio r (fraction of next-tracks served from catalogue) the effective per-track cost is 0.08 (1 - r), so at r = 0.9 the per-track cost is $0.008 and a $10 rewarded eCPM covers 1.25 tracks per view.

## Five-part academic records (each [FULL] primary)

### S08 — Huang, Reiley, Riabov, "Measuring Consumer Sensitivity to Audio Advertising: A Long-Run Field Experiment on Pandora Internet Radio", working paper, first version 2018-04-21, this version 2024-08-26 (arXiv 2412.05516v1). Read [FULL] from the author PDF text (capture 12a6e025…, 1358 lines; Sections 1–6, Tables 1–6, Figures 1–7).
1. Problem in the authors' framing: ad-supported digital media lack credible causal estimates of how listeners respond to ad load; existing evidence is observational, short-run and small-sample, and observational methods can be biased or even directionally wrong.
2. Method: randomised experiment on Pandora, June 2014–April 2016 (21 months of consistent assignment); 19 % of listeners randomised into nine 1 % treatment groups (pods/hour × ads/pod: 3, 4, 6 pods × 1, 1.5, 2 ads) plus a 10 % control at the status quo (= 4×1.5); manipulation applied to mobile-app listening only (~80 % of listening); outcomes: hours listened, active days, probability of being active in the final month, subscription; estimation by 2SLS with realised ads/hour instrumented by treatment dummies; elasticity = slope ÷ (1/3.622 = 27.6 % change per unit ad).
3. Real numbers: realised audio ads/hour by group 2.735–5.021 (control 3.628); pods/hour 2.65–3.53 (Table 3); highest group received 84 % more ads/hour than the lowest; +1 ad/hour → hours −2.082 % ± 0.227 %, active days −1.911 % ± 0.130 % (Table 5); decomposition: hours/active-day −0.4 %, days/active-listener −0.95 %, probability of being active −0.92 % (Table 6, sums to 2.27 %); long-run elasticities ≈ −0.070 (hours) and −0.076 (days) versus ≈ −0.02/−0.025 in month 1 (Figure 7); weekly active listeners in the highest group 2 % below control and lowest group 1 % above by the end (Figure 5); pre-treatment balance χ² tests pass at 5 % (Table 2).
4. Stated limitations: only mobile-app ads were manipulated, attenuating elasticities (footnote 2, Section 6); realised load differs from intended because not every ad opportunity is filled and session length varies; treatment ramped up over six weeks; results are for one US service in 2014–2016. Analyst-identified: Pandora is lean-back radio with 3–5 min tracks; PlayMusicPrompts tracks are ~60 s and generated on demand, so "ads per hour" not "ads per track" is the transferable unit; the subscription-uplift result does not transfer while PMP has no paid tier.
5. Application here: fixes the cadence unit for any audio or interstitial ad in the app and web player to ADS PER HOUR, sets the evidence-based operating band at 3–5/hour with a linear ~2 %-per-ad-per-hour cost on listening, argues for more frequent shorter breaks over long pods, and warns that a one-month A/B on PMP will understate long-run churn by ~3× — so the parent's ad experiments must run long and read early-skip/full-listen signals as leading indicators.
