Measured from the primaries: prior OPT-IN is required for BOTH analytics and personalised ads in **TR** (KVKK Kurul 2022/229: "opt-in … varsayılan ayar olarak çerezlerin çalışmaması", capture `2026-09-05-consent-kvkk-kurul-2022-229-tr-text.txt`), **EEA** (ePrivacy Art. 5(3) consolidated 2009 text: "has given his or her consent", capture `…eprivacy-2002-58-consolidated-text.txt`; EDPB 2/2023 v2.0 extends it to pixels, IP-only tracking and unique identifiers, `…edpb-guidelines-2-2023-art53-v2.txt` L35–L39), **UK** (PECR reg. 6 as substituted by DUAA 2025 s.112; personalised advertising still needs consent — the new Schedule A1 para 5 statistical exception is opt-OUT-with-notice only for first-party, non-shared statistics, `…uk-duaa-2025-sch12-scheduleA1.txt` L93–L101, in force 5 Feb 2026 L60), **CH for personalised ads** (nDSG high-risk profiling → opt-in per EDÖB Leitfaden 22 Jan 2025 + 6 Oct 2025 supplement §3.10.5/§3.11.3, `…ch-edoeb-cookie-leitfaden-2025-redline-text.txt` L15, L69–L74; plain cookies remain notice+opt-out under FMG Art. 45c), and **Quebec (CA)** (Law 25 s.8.1 tracking "deactivated by default" — `[single-source]` via law-firm/CAI secondary, primary text NOT captured). Google's own policy ADDS a constraint independent of law: a Google-certified TCF CMP is REQUIRED to serve personalised ads to EEA/UK/CH users (`google-eu-user-consent-policy-help.txt` L15) — the site's custom banner therefore cannot unlock personalised ads there regardless of the legal analysis.

**Committed recommendation (one).** Three-tier region table, decided server-side from `CloudFront-Viewer-Country` and on-device by UMP: **Tier A OPT-IN** (all four Consent Mode purposes default `denied`; Accept / Reject equal prominence; no pre-ticked toggles): TR, the 30 EEA states, GB, CH, and CA-QC when region is available (else all of CA). **Tier B OPT-OUT-ON** (defaults `granted`; visible "Privacy choices" + GPC honoured with on-screen confirmation): US, BR (analytics only — ads `denied` until consent), AU, JP, KR, IN, rest of world. **Tier C UNKNOWN** (no country header, header value `ZZ`/absent, or AWS-network origin): treat as Tier A — the only default lawful in every jurisdiction. Rationale for C from the primaries: Google itself documents that a default command *without* `region` is what governs "all other regions" (`tag-platform-consent-mode-setup.txt` L368) and that the most specific region wins (L389–L408); ISO 3166-2 sub-regions (US-CA, CA-QC) are supported there.
