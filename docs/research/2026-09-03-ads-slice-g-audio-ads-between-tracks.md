## Item 2 — Standards

- **VAST 4.x is the audio standard; DAAST 1.0 is deprecated.** VAST 4.3 §1.4: "DAAST … is now being merged back into VAST4"; the changes are the `adType` attribute on `<Ad>` (values per XSD annotation: "identifies the type of ad … support audio ad scenarios. The default value is video"; DAAST values video/audio/hybrid per S21 + secondary), "media player" wording, and per-element audio notes [S04 §1.4, lines 453–472 of capture]. §1.4.1 lists four audio player use cases: audio-only; audio + companion; audio delivered via a video creative played audio-only; video trafficked audio-first [S04]. §1.4.2: "Audibility"/Viewability — "Discussions are still ongoing about the concept of 'Audible Impression'" [S04].
- **MediaFile for audio**: `type` = MIME ("audio/mpeg", "audio/aac" named in the spec), `width`/`height` "0 for audio ads", `delivery` progressive|streaming, `bitrate` or `minBitrate`/`maxBitrate`, optional `codec` (RFC 4281), `fileSize`, `mediaType` [S04 §3.9.1, capture line 1290]. Three ready-to-serve files at high/medium/low quality are the VAST 4 guidance; a `<Mezzanine>` is required only for ad-stitched (SSAI) executions, error 406/407 when missing/transcoding [S04 §3.9.2].
- **Ad types relevant to audio**: Linear, Companion, Skippable Linear, Ad Pods — "all ad types are relevant for 'audio only' ads except NonLinear ads" [S04 §2.3.1, line 614]. `skipoffset` on `<Linear>` defines when skip controls appear; publisher may set a minimum and disregard lower values [S04 §3.8].
- **Duration**: `<Duration>` required, HH:MM:SS(.mmm) [S04 §3.8.1]. **Expires**: integer seconds after which a pre-fetched ad must not be played [S04 §3.4.10] — directly useful for our "prefetch the ad at track START, play at boundary" flow.
- **ViewableImpression**: "not applicable for Audio use cases" [S04 §3.5, line 1128]. **Icons**: optional for audio, may live in the companion [S04 line 826]. **Impression**: URIs with the same `id` fired together; XSD: "the media player must use to notify the ad server when the impression occurs" [S21].
- **Tracking events**: the descriptive table (§3.14.1) was NOT in the extracted text of 4.1/4.2/4.3 (pages 54–80 dropped) — `[PARTIAL]`. What the XSD annotations (S21) and the IAB 4.1 blog (Q2 result 2) establish: linear events start/firstQuartile/midpoint/thirdQuartile/complete, mute/unmute, pause/resume, skip, `progress` with an `offset` attribute "Must be present for progress event", `creativeView` for companions ("the only supported event"); for audio "events like starts and quartiles can be validly fired even if the media player is not visible" (IAB blog, official). The dailymotion vast-client-js tracker (S20, code, Apache-2.0 — licence read from repo header not verified this session `[UNVERIFIED]`) implements exactly this event set for the web.
- **AdVerifications**: `<Verification vendor="company.com-omid">` with JavaScriptResource/ExecutableResource, executed BEFORE creative playback; if none can run, fire `verificationNotExecuted` [S21 annotations]. OMID API 1.3 (2022-06-22) "Audio ad support: New creative type added for audio and new rules to support audio measurement" [S30 changelog].
- **Macros (VAST 4.1+)**: `[REGULATIONS]` (values coppa, gdpr) and `[GDPRCONSENT]` ("Base64-encoded Cookie Value of IAB GDPR consent info") apply to "All tracking pixels VAST request URIs" [S05 §6, capture lines 2490–2503]; `[PODSEQUENCE]`, `[BREAKMAXADS]` and other ad-break macros exist [S05 lines 1917, 2388]. `US_PRIVACY`/GPP macros: not located in the extracted text — `[UNVERIFIED]` for VAST; GAM's tag parameters page covers them (S02, not read in full).
- **VMAP 1.0.1** (IAB, 2014): `<vmap:AdBreak timeOffset=… breakType="linear" breakId=…>` with `<vmap:AdSource>` → `<vmap:AdTagURI templateType="vast3">` or inline `<VASTAdData>`; `timeOffset` grammar hh:mm:ss[.mmm], n%, start, end, #m [S29 PDF text nearly empty; grammar confirmed by S31 Google Cloud example and vastlint secondary]. For PMP the break schedule is decided by our queue, not by a VMAP document, so VMAP is optional; it becomes relevant only for SSAI (Item 3d).
- **OpenRTB 2.6 `Object: Audio`** (§3.2.8): `mimes` string array REQUIRED; `minduration`/`maxduration` (mutually exclusive with `rqddurs` exact durations for live radio); `startdelay` per AdCOM Start Delay Modes (0 pre-roll, −1 mid-roll, −2 post-roll); pod fields `podid`, `podseq`, `slotinpod`, `maxseq`; `stitched`, `nvol`, `companiontype`, `delivery`; companions as `Banner[]` [S06 FULL section]. Relevant only when we sell through an SSP; recorded for the parent.
- **MRC Digital Audio Measurement Standards v1.0**: Audible Ad Impression = client-side records, filtered for IVT, excluding ads served in a known player-muted state (with non-zero volume) and ads audible < 2 continuous seconds; "muting at the device level is difficult … not required to account for at the present time" [S07 PARTIAL].
- **IAB Podcast Measurement Technical Guidelines 2.x**: not read this session (Q12 returned no primary) — applicability to a streaming music player is `[UNVERIFIED]`; they measure downloads by byte threshold, which does not fit our streamed WAV/Opus model (secondary S-rtblint) — absence recorded.
- **ads.txt / app-ads.txt / sellers.json for audio**: the GAM audio page does not mention them; AdTonos requires "Configured ads.txt" for its integrations [S10]; S-E already covers Google's ads.txt line. No audio-specific IAB guidance read — gap.
- **TCF v2.2 in VAST**: consent travels via `[GDPRCONSENT]`/`[REGULATIONS]` macros (S05) and, for AdTonos, via the SDK `start(consent)` flag (S12).
- **SSAI vs client-side**: VAST 4 explicitly supports "ad-stitching" for players "that cannot execute and track ads"; in SSAI the publisher server fires tracking "concurrently with the corresponding playback events" and forwards user agent etc. [S04 §1.1 / line 696]; `<Mezzanine>` becomes mandatory [S04 §3.9.2].

## Item 3 — Technical integration for THIS project

(a) **Ad-break item in the queue.** Queue entries in `deploy/payload/public/site/session.js` and `app/lib/src/state/listening_session.dart` gain a discriminated type `{kind: "track"} | {kind: "adBreak"}` (naming is the parent's, per the project's OO standards). An `adBreak` is INSERTED ONLY between two tracks (Berk's algorithm fires generation at track START; mid-track and the T−90 s recalibration are content time — S-E Item 4, AdMob S03 "logical breaks"). Eligibility rule (CALCULATION from S-E §S08 / S-E1 §S25): `listenedSecondsSinceLastBreak ≥ 720–1200 s` (3–5 breaks/hour, Pandora band 2.7–5.0, control 3.63) AND previous track reached `FULL_LISTEN (≥90 %)` AND NOT `EARLY_SKIP (<10 s)` AND buffer has ≥1 prepared take (so the ad never masks a generation stall). Pod length 1 (Pandora Table 9: pod of 1 = −1.966 vs pod of 2 = −4.116 hours effect, S-E1). Frequency cap stored per `anonId`/`userId` in Postgres and mirrored in local state so a reload cannot reset it.
(b) **Web (`<audio>`).** Fetch the VAST URL with `fetch()` at track START of the track preceding the break (prefetch), parse XML (`DOMParser`), follow `<Wrapper>` chains (limit per spec, error 302), pick `<MediaFile type="audio/mpeg"|"audio/aac"|"audio/mp4">` playable by `audio.canPlayType`, honour `<Expires>` [S04 §3.4.10]. Play the ad in the SAME hidden `<audio>` element: MDN — autoplay blocking applies to audible media started programmatically in a tab with no prior user interaction; since the listener already pressed play (sticky activation), `audio.play()` at the boundary resolves; still handle the rejected `play()` promise with `NotAllowedError` → show a play control [S32 FULL]. CORS: ad MediaFiles are cross-origin; do not attach `crossorigin` (no Web Audio processing on the ad) so the media loads as opaque; tracking pixels are `Image()`/`navigator.sendBeacon` fire-and-forget. Fire `<Impression>` on first `playing`, `start` on first non-zero `timeupdate`, quartiles at 25/50/75 % of `<Duration>`, `complete` on `ended`, `mute`/`unmute` on `volumechange`, `skip` on user skip after `skipoffset`, each once (S20 pattern; S21 event names). Companion: render `<Companion>` StaticResource (300×250 / 320×50) in the player panel during the break, fire `creativeView` [S21]. No-ad (empty `<VAST>` or `<Error>` only): fire the `<Error>` URI with `[ERRORCODE]` where applicable and advance to the next track immediately; the listener never hears silence.
(c) **Flutter (just_audio 0.10.6, published ~2026-07 [S19]).** Playlist API is now `player.setAudioSources(list, useLazyPreparation: true)`, `addAudioSource`, `insertAudioSource(index, source)` (ConcatenatingAudioSource is deprecated in favour of these) [S19 lines 38, 93–105]. Insert `AudioSource.uri(adMediaFileUrl, tag: AdBreakTag(...))` at the boundary index; quartiles from `positionStream` against the VAST `<Duration>`; `currentIndexStream`/`sequenceStateStream` detect ad start/end [S19 line 229–233]; tracking pixels via `http` GET. Background/lock-screen: `just_audio_background`/`audio_service` keep playback alive [S19 line 404–405]; VAST 4.1 (IAB blog) says audio starts/quartiles are valid when the player is not visible, and AdTonos requires `pauseAd` when the foreground is lost [S12] — so per-network policy differs: with our own VAST (house ads) background playout is permitted by the standard; with AdTonos it must pause; AdMob interstitials never play in background (S-E S03). Companion: a Flutter widget shown only while foregrounded. Consent: UMP/ATT flow from S-E Item 2 runs before any personalised ad request; pass the TCF string into `[GDPRCONSENT]` or the SDK consent flag (S05, S12).
(d) **SSAI alternative.** Stitch the ad into the served audio on the CloudFront/S3 path (slice A): pros — one continuous stream, immune to client ad-blockers, works with any player; cons — VAST requires a `<Mezzanine>` for stitched executions and transcoding [S04 §3.9.2], tracking must be fired server-side "concurrently with the corresponding playback events" with client headers forwarded [S04 line 696], quartile/skip fidelity is lost, and our catalogue is WAV masters (5.7–16.8 MB) — concatenating an MP3/AAC ad into a WAV or Opus stream needs a transcode step per session. Verdict: client-side insertion first; SSAI only if ad-blocking or a vendor requires it. `[INFERENCE]`
(e) **Measurement into Postgres.** Table (name is the parent's) with one row per ad opportunity: `anonId/userId, sessionId, boundaryIndex, vastAdId/AdServingId, creativeId (UniversalAdId), impressionAt, quartiles[], completedAt, skippedAt, audibleSeconds (volume>0 & !muted, MRC ≥2 s), companionViewed, errorCode, source ("house"|"adtonos"|"gam")`. Duplicate suppression: fire each event once per (adServingId, event); MRC audible impression = not muted AND volume > 0 AND ≥ 2 continuous seconds [S07]. Reconcile daily against the network's reporting API (AdTonos portal / GAM reports "1x1v" size [S01]).

## Item 4 — Listener experience and policy

- **Disclosure**: DSA Art. 26 requires each ad to be identifiable as an ad with the advertiser and payer named (S-E S58/S59; S-E1 notes the Art. 19 micro-enterprise exemption as single-source). Practical rule: a 1–2 s spoken or displayed "Advertisement" cue before the break (house intro) plus the companion label. This is also the pattern AdTonos requires for radio ("intro and outro" markers) [S11].
- **Loudness**: EBU R 128 s1 — adverts normalised to −23.0 LUFS ±0.5 LU, −1 dBTP, max short-term −18 LUFS (or max momentary −15 LUFS), LRA not applicable to short-form [S08 FULL]. AES TD1008 (2021) and AES77-2023 are the streaming recommendations; EBU R128 s2 (2021/2023) is the streaming supplement [S18 table]. PMP masters are −14 LUFS / −1 dBTP (CONTEXT-03). Rule: measure each ad file's integrated loudness (BS.1770-5 gated) and apply a gain so the ad sits at −14 LUFS ±1 LU with −1 dBTP ceiling — in the browser via a gain node is impossible without CORS-clean media, so apply the gain SERVER-SIDE (proxy the MediaFile through our origin and normalise once, cache by creative id) — `[INFERENCE]` from S08 + S32 + CONTEXT-03.
- **Skip norms**: Spotify first-party ad-skip policy: NOT found in any primary read this session (also not found by slice E) — `[UNVERIFIED]`; do not quote a number. VAST gives the mechanism (`skipoffset`) and Pandora's RCT gives the tolerance curve.
- **Consent**: personalised audio ads in EEA/UK/CH require a certified TCF CMP (S-E S01); KVKK: advertising cookies/identifiers need açık rıza opt-in on web AND apps (S-E S60/S61). Contextual (non-personalised) house audio needs no consent signal but still needs the ads.txt/privacy-policy disclosures.
- **EU AI Act Art. 50** (applies from 2026-08-02; grace to 2026-12-02 only for 50(2) marking of pre-existing systems) [S26 FULL]: 50(2) marking is the PROVIDER's duty (the music engine's vendor); 50(4) deployer disclosure attaches to DEEPFAKES (content resembling existing persons/places/events) and to AI text on public-interest matters — our generated music is not a deepfake unless it imitates a real artist, and the ad is human-made, so no Art. 50 label is owed for the ad break itself; a general "AI-generated music" disclosure on the product remains good practice and the Code of Practice proposes an "AI" icon and "short audible disclaimer" for audio [S25, S27 secondary]. `[INFERENCE]` on applicability; the legal call is Berk's.

## Item 5 — Revenue and break-even

Primary numbers: US digital audio ad revenue $8.4 B (2025, +10.2 %), podcast $2.9 B (+17.6 %), audio share of internet ad revenue 2.8 % [S09]. Spotify Q2 2026: ad-supported revenue €446 M, 494 M ad-supported MAUs, automated channels ≈40 % of ad revenue [S17 secondary restating the 6-K] → €0.90 per ad-supported MAU per quarter ≈ €0.30/month (CALCULATION). Triton/CBAA: $0.70 CPM platform fee on direct-sold; 60 % publisher share on programmatic [S16]. No audio CPM benchmark for a small non-US publisher exists in any source read; Türkiye audio CPM: none found (absence).
Break-even (CALCULATION, inputs sourced): cost per generated take $0.08 (CONTEXT-03). With B breaks/hour at eCPM e and fill f, hourly revenue R = B·e·f/1000. At the Pandora band B = 4: e = $5, f = 0.5 → $0.010/hour; e = $15, f = 0.8 → $0.048/hour. Takes consumed per hour at ~60 s tracks ≈ 60 without reuse → $4.80/hour cost. Ads cover 0.2–1 % of fresh generation. With reuse ratio r (slices A–D) the cost is 4.80·(1−r): at r = 0.95 → $0.24/hour, ads cover 4–20 %; at r = 0.99 → $0.048/hour → break-even at the optimistic cell. Conclusion: audio ads between tracks are a REVENUE LINE only when reuse carries ≥95–99 % of next-tracks; at launch they are a house-ad/sponsor channel and a measurement pipeline, not the cost cover. Falsifier: a measured audio eCPM ≥ $40 with fill ≥ 0.8 at 4/hour ($0.128/hour) would cover fresh generation at r ≥ 0.973.
