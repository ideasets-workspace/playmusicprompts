## Ad-supported tier mechanics — the quantitative anchor is now READ (A-06, Goli–Huang–Reiley–Riabov 2024, [FULL])
Pandora's own randomized experiment (34,390,962 listeners, 21 months): one additional audio ad per hour → **−2.08 % ± 0.23 % listening hours and −1.91 % ± 0.13 % active days** in the long run, linear across 2.7–5.0 realized ads/hour (Table 5, Fig. 6); long-run elasticity ≈ 3× the first-month estimate (Fig. 7); at equal load listeners **weakly prefer more, shorter breaks** (2β1−β2 = 0.41, p<0.05 for active days; Table 9); each extra ad/hour raises paid-subscriber probability by only +0.145 pp (≈0.75 ¢/listener/month at $4.99) while raising churn +0.336 pp (Table 10). Application to the owner's 3–5 breaks/h design: inside the measured band; expect ≈ −2 % hours per extra break/hour, ~80 % of the loss via fewer sessions/active days rather than shorter sessions; a one-ad-per-break shape is the favoured pod length; do not use ad load as the upsell lever.
